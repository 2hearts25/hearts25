## Closeout items

1. Reconcile the affirmative emergency transportation grant and the applicable DCY 01234-equivalent record. Verify that required child-specific notes are available wherever the center uses or carries that record.
2. Clarify the modified-diet threshold, per-medication documentation, conscience exemption scope, and behavior-trigger question. Obtain the approved duration, withdrawal and identifying-information terms for media consent and an explicit reviewed-with-staff receipt workflow when required.
3. Issue the handbook separately when approved. Continue using event-specific field-trip permission, center sign-off and annual review, and practitioner-signed supporting records as applicable. Those are distinct obligations; online intake does not perform them.
4. Preserve the established outputs: exactly two generated PDFs, with validated attachments separate in the authorized administration email workflow. Parent access is limited to those two PDFs. This audit makes no release-activation or production-publication determination.
