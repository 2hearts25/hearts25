# Absent Days and Attendance Policy — APPROVED text (management, 11/24/2026)

Regular attendance supports children’s learning, development and participation in our programs.

Parents and guardians are asked to notify Heart to Heart Enrichment Center as early as possible when their child will be absent due to illness, family circumstances, appointments or other reasons.

**Publicly Funded Child Care (PFCC)**
Families receiving PFCC are subject to applicable Ohio attendance and authorization requirements. Under current Ohio PFCC rules, children may be eligible for up to 20 reimbursable absent days during each six-month period, January through June and July through December. Heart to Heart will work with families to understand applicable attendance requirements and address extended or recurring absences.

**Extended Absences**
Families anticipating an extended absence should contact the Director to discuss their circumstances and available arrangements. Any applicable financial obligations will be addressed separately through the approved Parent/Provider Contract and applicable program requirements.

**Parent Acknowledgment**
“I acknowledge that I have received and reviewed the Heart to Heart Enrichment Center Absent Days and Attendance Policy and understand my responsibility to communicate with the center regarding my child’s attendance.”
